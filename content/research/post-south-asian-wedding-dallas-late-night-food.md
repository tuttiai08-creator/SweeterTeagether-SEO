## SERP pattern

- Generic Dallas late-night snack / dessert-truck queries are saturated by **Mr Sugar Rush** near-duplicate blogs (on-site dates include 17 Jun, 2 Aug, 17 Aug, 23 Aug 2026).
- South Asian late-night treat / chaat queries surface Tiffins, Ekta’s Kitchen, Richi. **Only Tiffins prints “late-night chaat station.”** **Walima** did not appear on those operator pages.
- Boba hire pages exist (The Boba Plug, Fat Straws, Ellie & Juice, Magic Cup). None verified as a late-night wedding send-off. `bobabarexperience.com` **404**.
- Hotel “after-party catering” SERP = hotel wedding landings, not a third-party cart category.
- False positives: Maddilicious is Riverside, CA. Hello Boba Truck is Illinois.

## Verified operators (own sites, 2026-08-27)

**Sweet, labeled late-night**
- **Bite & Bloom** (`/weddings`, `/minipancakes`): earlier parent-file timeout is **resolved**. Carts: mini pancake, Dubai chocolate strawberries, soft serve, churro, dirty soda, **fresh fruit bar**, s’mores. Explicit “late-night wedding snacks.” Lists mehndi, sangeet, nikkah. **No chaat, boba, or cookie skillet.** No dollar packages published.
- **Iced Out:** “Late-Night Dessert Bar / Service” after dinner and dancing. Water ice. No prices on fetched pages.
- **Mr Sugar Rush:** late-night dessert truck (already in parent file).
- **Allure Bites:** ice cream / charcuterie / “late-night treats.” No homepage prices.
- **Tongue in Cheek** truck rates as of 3.31.2024 on own book page ($200/30 min up to 50 scoops through $500/2 hr). Page does **not** say wedding or late-night. On Dallas Arboretum approved specialty-food list.

**Savory chaat (wedding station; late-night only if named)**
- **Tiffins ToGo:** still the only fetched operator that prints late-night chaat on sangeet and reception. Events named do **not** include walima.
- **Ekta’s Kitchen** services page: Live Chaat Station, min 40 guests, 4.5 hours. Extra chaat **$2.50/person** (theirs). Wedding is a use case. **Does not say late-night.**
- **Richi:** live chaat/dosa/tandoor/dessert upon request. No late-night or walima wording.
- Chaat **cart** as a distinct product: Desi Cart / Peacock Elite / Sigree directory-only — unverified.

**Boba hire (not labeled late-night)**
- The Boba Plug (weddings, ~2 weeks notice), Fat Straws (weddings; boba + mochi donuts), Magic Cup (weddings/receptions), Ellie & Juice (Dallas page does not name weddings). Do not invent ST packages from this set.

**Cookie skillet:** no DFW mobile wedding cart on an own site. D-Spot Carrollton is a **cafe**.

## Venue F&B gates

- **Dallas Palms:** open vendor (own outside-catering pages). Extra cart is plausible.
- **Dallas Arboretum** (2024–2026 Wedding Guide PDF, uploaded Feb 2025): approved caterer list; specialty food is still an **approved list**, not any truck.
- **The Joule** (Apr 2025 banquet PDF): “**Joule is the sole provider of all food and beverage served in the event space.**” Post-reception room: Midnight Rambler. In-house dessert action stations. Indian Spice buffet samosa chaat is an **appetizer**, not late-night. Hard cart blocker.
- **Ritz Dallas:** approved outside culinary partner only (already in parent file).
- **Hotel ZaZa:** late-night language is bar hours; outside-food policy not on the fetched page.

## Honest ST read (category, not a case study)

Venue filter is the constraint, not whether DFW receptions want late-night snacks. Open halls can take a cart. Hotels usually cannot.

- **Boba:** hireable in DFW; not labeled late-night on competitor pages. Closer to Palms “Goodnight” coffee/sundae than to chaat.
- **Cookie skillet:** format gap, not a documented guest-request SERP.
- **Fruit chaat:** savory late-night chaat already has Tiffins. Bite & Bloom fruit bar is not chaat. Do not present “after elders leave” as published local behavior.
- **Walima** absent from operator pages this pass. Do not invent a post-walima food ritual.
