# Content Clusters

Clusters group future articles so internal links and coverage stay coherent. **Do not fill these with large topic lists yet.** Add topics only after scoring (TOPIC_SCORING.md) and a dedicated research pass.

## How a cluster works

Each cluster has:

- **Audience** — who the pages are for
- **Commercial job** — how this cluster should lead to an inquiry
- **Hub vs. spoke** — one eventual pillar / hub page plus supporting articles (hubs are *planned*, not written yet)
- **Admission criteria** — what must be true to add a topic
- **Topic log** — empty until research begins

When adding a topic later, record: working title, target query, intent layer (from CONTENT_STRATEGY.md), score, and status (`idea` → `researched` → `draft` → `reviewed` → `published`).

## Adding topics later (checklist)

1. Score the candidate (TOPIC_SCORING.md). Prefer topics that clear the minimum bar there.
2. Assign **one** primary cluster. Note secondary clusters for internal links only.
3. Confirm it does not duplicate an existing idea in another cluster.
4. Add a one-line log entry (title, query, score, status). Do not write the article in this file.
5. Research and draft in `content/` per AGENTS.md.

## Cross-linking (when content exists)

- Direct catering pages ↔ all event clusters
- Dallas weddings ↔ South Asian weddings where the reader overlap is real
- Showers / engagement ↔ weddings (planning sequence, not keyword stuffing)
- Inspiration pieces only link into a cluster if the intent truly leads there
